# ByteQuest

## Language

**Workshop lesson**

An instructor’s PDF shared with one Workshop for its members to read and study.

**PDF attachment**

A PDF selected by a student as the study source for a temporary Chat Assistant conversation.

**Workshop language**

The programming language a Workshop teaches. A student's languages are the Workshop languages of the Workshops they belong to; students never choose one separately.
_Avoid_: track, language track, enrolled language

**Workshop activity**

A coding challenge or concept check quiz that a Workshop shares with its members. It is the only way an activity reaches a student, apart from course-only activities.
_Avoid_: track practice, quest (for the shared item)

**Class leaderboard**

The XP ranking of one Workshop's members against each other.
_Avoid_: track leaderboard

**Next up**

The one activity a student is recommended to do next across all their Workshops: the open, unfinished Workshop activity due soonest.
_Avoid_: next quest on your track

**Pass mark**

The score an attempt must reach for its activity to count as passed. It is set per activity; a coding challenge with none set must pass every test.
_Avoid_: passing grade, cutoff (cutoff is the prediction threshold)

**Activities passed**

How many of the activities a student can open they have passed at least once, out of all of them.
_Avoid_: attempts passed, completion rate

**First-try pass**

An activity whose first graded attempt reached its pass mark.
_Avoid_: first-attempt success

**Takeaway**

The one sentence above a chart or metric that says what it shows, written by fixed rules from the data.
_Avoid_: insight, summary (summary is the screen-reader description)
