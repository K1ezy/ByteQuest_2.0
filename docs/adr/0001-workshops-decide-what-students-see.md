# Workshops, not a language track, decide what a student sees

Instructors teach several languages at once, and students take more than one of those classes, so a single language chosen per student ("track") blocked real enrolments and confused students. A student now sees and opens only Workshop activities from the Workshops they belong to, in any Workshop language (course-only activities keep their course-membership rule), and nobody chooses a language separately. XP and level are one total per student; leaderboards rank one Workshop's members; Memory Check concepts stay separate per language; the AI tutor uses the language of the activity in front of the student.

## Considered Options

- **Keep one track and let students switch it**: still one language at a time, and switching would hide the other classes' work.
- **Several tracks per student**: the same idea under a new name; the Workshop already says which language a class uses.
- **Workshops plus open practice in any language**: rejected because almost every activity is already released through a Workshop, and an open catalogue brings back the "Track practice" confusion.

## Consequences

- An archived Workshop, or one the student leaves, stops new attempts on its activities; past attempts, XP, stars and badges stay.
- Students in no active Workshop see only their history and course-only activities.
- The stored per-student language is no longer read. It stays in the database until a later cleanup, because research exports may still read it.
